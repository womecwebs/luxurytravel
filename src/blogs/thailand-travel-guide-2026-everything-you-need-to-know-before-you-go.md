## Quick Facts About Thailand

* **Region:** Southeast Asia
* **Capital:** Bangkok
* **Currency:** Thai Baht (THB)
* **Language:** Thai (English widely spoken in tourist areas)
* **Visa:** Visa-free or visa-on-arrival for many nationalities
* **Best For:** First-time travelers, families, solo travelers, luxury travel, wellness, culture, beaches

## Entry Requirements & Visas for Thailand (2026)

Thailand offers **one of the easiest entry systems** in Asia.

### Visa Overview

* Many travelers receive **visa-free entry (30–60 days)** depending on nationality
* Visa-on-arrival available for select countries
* Long-stay, retirement, and digital nomad-style visas are available through official channels

As of January 1, 2025, Thailand requires most visa applications to be submitted through their official [e-Visa platform](https://www.thaievisa.go.th/). *Tourists from many countries can enter visa-free for up to 60 days, while others may require a Tourist (TR) visa, which offers 60-day stays extendable by 30 days*. A passport with at least 6 months' validity is required. So you can check the  [e-Visa platform](https://www.thaievisa.go.th/) for more detailes for specific, up-to-date requirements.[](https://www.thaievisa.go.th/)

### What You Typically Need

* Passport valid for at least **6 months**
* Proof of onward travel (sometimes requested)
* Travel insurance (strongly recommended)
* Proof of accommodation (for some visa types)

**Important:** Entry rules can change. Always confirm with official Thai immigration sources before departure.

## Best Time to Visit Thailand (Month-by-Month Insight)

Thailand is a year-round destination, but timing affects **weather, crowds, and costs**.

### Seasons Explained

**Cool Season (November – February)**

* Best overall weather
* Dry, comfortable temperatures
* Peak travel season

**Hot Season (March – May)**

* Very hot, especially in cities
* Fewer crowds, lower hotel prices

**Green Season (June – October)**

* Short tropical rains
* Lush landscapes
* Best value and fewer tourists

**Best overall time:** November to February\
**Best value:** June to October

## Top Destinations in Thailand (What Each Is Best For)

Thailand is not one destination—it’s many, and each region serves a different purpose.

### Bangkok

* Culture, temples, food, nightlife
* Transport hub
* Best for first-time visitors

### Chiang Mai & Northern Thailand

* Mountains, temples, slow travel
* Cultural depth
* Best for wellness and long stays

### Phuket

* Beaches, luxury resorts, nightlife
* Best infrastructure for international travelers

### Krabi & Railay

* Dramatic landscapes, island hopping
* Best for nature-focused trips

### Koh Samui & Gulf Islands

* Wellness retreats, yoga, quieter beaches
* Best for couples and long-stay travelers

## How Much Does a Trip to Thailand Cost?

Thailand works for almost every budget.

### Budget Travel

* $40–70 per day
* Guesthouses, street food, public transport

### Mid-Range Travel

* $80–150 per day
* Comfortable hotels, tours, domestic flights

### Luxury Travel

* $250+ per day
* [Private transfers](https://kiwitaxi.tpk.mx/7OgDoKGT), luxury resorts, curated experiences

Thailand offers one of the **best price-to-quality ratios** in global travel.

## Safety, Scams & Cultural Etiquette

Thailand is generally very safe, but awareness matters.

### Common Issues to Avoid

### Cultural Etiquette

* Dress modestly at temples
* Remove shoes when required
* Respect the monarchy and religion

Thailand values politeness—basic respect goes a long way.
