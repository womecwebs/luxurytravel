faqs:
  - question: What are the cheapest months to travel internationally?
    answer: The cheapest months to travel are typically January, February, and
      November, excluding major holidays. These months often fall outside peak
      tourist seasons, resulting in lower flight and hotel prices.
  - question: What is the best month to travel overall?
    answer: There is no single best month for all destinations. The best month to
      travel depends on weather patterns, crowd levels, and your travel goals.
      For many regions, March, May, and September offer the best balance of good
      weather and lower prices.
  - question: Why does the month matter when planning a trip?
    answer: The month determines weather conditions, travel costs, and tourist
      crowds. Visiting a destination in the wrong season can mean extreme heat,
      heavy rain, or inflated prices. Planning by month helps avoid these
      problems and improves the overall travel experience.
  - question: What is the best month for budget travel?
    answer: January, February, and November are generally the best months for budget
      travel due to lower demand, discounted flights, and reduced accommodation
      prices.
  - question: Which months are best for beach vacations?
    answer: |-
      The best months for beach travel depend on the region:

      November to March: Southeast Asia, Caribbean

      May to September: Europe and the Mediterranean

      June to August: Northern Hemisphere coastal destinations

      Always consider hurricane and monsoon seasons.
  - question: Can AI help me decide where to travel next?
    answer: Yes. AI-powered travel tools analyze seasonality, weather patterns, and
      travel preferences to recommend destinations that make sense for your
      timing and goals, reducing decision fatigue.
